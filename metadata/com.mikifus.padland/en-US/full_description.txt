Padland is an Android app to manage, organize and open collaborative documents based on Etherpad and CryptPad.

Main features:
- Add custom servers
- Organize documents in groups
- Compatible with Etherpad and CryptPad servers
- Import and export your database
- Sort and bulk-select pads
- Long press the new pad button to import from the clipboard
- Offline mode support

Padland helps you keep collaborative documents together in one place while staying simple and lightweight.
